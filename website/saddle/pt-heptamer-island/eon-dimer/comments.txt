<p>This entry uses a min-mode following method to locate saddle points.
The minimum mode was estimated with the dimer method. The dimer 
was rotated until the angle of rotation was smaller than 5 degrees or 
10 rotations had been made.</p>

<p>The rotational and translational optimization problems were solved using
conjugate-gradients. The step size was determined by taking a Newton step along
the search direction using a finite difference step size of 0.01 Angstrom. No
atom was allowed to move further than a distance of 0.2 Angstroms in a single
step.</p>
