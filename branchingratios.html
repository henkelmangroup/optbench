Accelerated MD

Accelerated MD Benchmarks for Escape Rates and Branching Ratios.

Adatom on Al(100) Surface

This benchmark tests accelerated MD methods by calculating product branching ratios. A tar file containing the minimized reactant state is found here: reactant.tar.gz.

The benchmark requires 5000 sample products are which obtained using an accelerated MD method at 250K. Either the Langevin thermostat with a friction coefficient between 0.005 and 0.03 or the Anderson thermostat with a collision strength between 0.2 and 0.8 and a collision period between 200 and 1000 fs should be used.
