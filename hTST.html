hTST Escape Rate Benchmarks

hTST escape rate benchmarks for single end point saddle searching algorithms.

These benchmarks test the performances of single end point saddle searching algorithms of finding all the saddles below certain barrier height, or reaching 99.9% of the true hTST rate assuming a constant prefactor of 1.2e+12 for each process. The maximum force on any atom has to be reduced to 0.001 eV/A for any successful searching.

Heptamer Island on Pt(111) Surface

All the 15 saddles below 1.3 eV are required to be found for this benchmark. The barrier heights are: 0.601, 0.601, 0.620, 0.986, 0.986, 0.987, 0.987, 0.989, 0.989, 1.196, 1.196, 1.196, 1.196, 1.207, 1.207 eV. The rates are calculated at 700K.
